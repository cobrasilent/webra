## ✨ Why Webra?

Most browsers collect your data, show you ads, and bury you in cookie pop-ups. Webra does the opposite: it blocks all of that by default and keeps everything on your own computer.

<br>

## 🚀 Features

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>🛡️ Built-in ad blocker</h3>
      Ads and trackers are blocked out of the box. No extension needed.
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>🌍 Full-page translation</h3>
      Translate entire web pages with a single click, right in the browser.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td valign="top">
      <h3>🔒 No account, no tracking</h3>
      Your data stays local. You never have to sign in.
    </td>
    <td valign="top">
      <h3>🍪 No cookie banners</h3>
      Annoying cookie prompts are cleared away automatically.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td valign="top">

      You decide if and when updates are installed.
